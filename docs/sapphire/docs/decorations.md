# Decorations

## Decoration browser

The browser lists decorations grouped by tag in collapsible folders, or as a thumbnail grid.

- **`+ Decoration`** picks the type: image, text, object, particle or component.
- **Duplicate** copies the selection, and **Delete** removes it.
- **Selecting a row** opens the decoration inspector in its own floating, resizable, dockable window. Closing it also clears the selection, and the browser list itself never reflows.

## Particles and objects

Particle and object decorations are fully editable.

- **Gradient colours** — modes for a single colour, two colours, a gradient, two gradients, or random, with colour-stop and alpha-stop editors.
- **Ranges** — float-pair and velocity-range fields.
- **Preview** — Play, Stop and Restart for particles.
- **Objects** expose their full property set, including the rows that depend on `objectType`.

Every colour here opens the [colour wheel](level-settings.md#colour-wheel).
